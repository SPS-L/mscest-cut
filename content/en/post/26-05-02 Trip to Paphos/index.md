MScEST students spent a full day exploring the UNESCO World Heritage city of Paphos and the surrounding region.

<!--more-->

On Saturday, 2 May 2026, students of the MSc in Electronic Science and Technology took a day trip to **Paphos**, discovering more than 3,000 years of Greek, Roman, and Byzantine history. Stops along the way included:

🏛️ **Kourion Archaeological Site** — the ancient Greco-Roman cliff-top city and its 2nd-century theatre overlooking the Mediterranean

🌊 **Petra tou Romiou** — the legendary birthplace of Aphrodite

🖼️ **Paphos Archaeological Park** — a UNESCO World Heritage Site, home to the remarkably preserved Roman mosaics of the Houses of Dionysos and Theseus

⚓ **Pafos Limanaki** — the medieval harbour, with free time for lunch at the waterfront tavernas beneath Paphos Castle

A great day of culture, history, and community away from the lab. See the full itinerary [here](https://apetros.github.io/mscest-paphos-2026/).
