## Overview

The MScEST is a **3-year, 120 ECTS** postgraduate programme jointly delivered
by CUT and HDU. It follows a **1+1+1 year structure** — coursework in China,
research and thesis in Cyprus, and an applied research and training year — and
educates engineering specialists able to conduct independent research and
manage engineering projects in a bilingual environment.

## Programme Structure

The programme spans three academic years (1+1+1):

| Year | Focus | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Year 1** | Coursework — 7 courses delivered by CUT staff and 6 by HDU staff; thesis proposal prepared with CUT staff at year end | HDU — Hangzhou, China |
| **Year 2** | Scientific research and the Master's thesis (30 ECTS); Research Methods course (EEN515); thesis mid-examination | CUT — Limassol, Cyprus |
| **Year 3** | Practical scientific and research training (30 ECTS) — extending the thesis with publication(s) or a patent, industry internships, and dissertation finalisation and defence | HDU — Hangzhou, China |

## Curriculum (120 ECTS)

| Component | ECTS |
|---|---|
| Compulsory courses (10) | 36 |
| Elective courses (3) | 22 |
| Postgraduate Thesis (Year 2) | 30 |
| Practical scientific and research training (Year 3) | 30 |
| Seminars by invited industry and academic speakers (Years 2–3) | 2 |
| **Total** | **120** |

## Key Facts

- **Duration:** 3 years (1+1+1), 120 ECTS
- **Language of instruction:** English
- **Degree awarded:** Master of Science in Electronics Science and Technology
- **Admission:** A recognised university degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering and Informatics or a related discipline (minimum cumulative average 6.5/10), plus proof of English proficiency
